# Pricing Strategy
**LinkedIn Hashtag Refresh Engine**

---

## Pricing Tiers

### Free Plan - $0/forever

**Target:** Users testing the platform, occasional posters

**Features:**
- 10 hashtag generations per month
- Basic AI suggestions
- Copy to clipboard
- Email support
- No credit card required

**Conversion triggers:**
- Hit generation limit
- Need more frequent posting
- Desire advanced features

---

### Pro Plan - $9/month ⭐

**Target:** Active LinkedIn creators, professionals, small teams

**Features:**
- **Unlimited** hashtag generations
- Advanced AI suggestions
- Priority generation speed
- Hashtag analytics & insights
- Bulk processing (unlimited posts)
- Team collaboration (3 users)
- Custom hashtag templates
- Priority email support
- Future: LinkedIn auto-posting
- Future: API access

**Value Proposition:**
- Saves 20+ minutes per post
- ROI: 4,500%+ (time saved vs cost)
- $9/month = ~$0.30/day
- Unlimited removes all usage anxiety

## Pricing Strategy

### Why This Works

1. **Simple Choice** - Free or Pro (no decision paralysis)
2. **Psychological Pricing** - $9 (below $10 barrier)
3. **Unlimited Model** - Removes usage anxiety
4. **High Margin** - Sustainable & scalable
5. **Broad Appeal** - Serves individuals to small teams

### Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Low conversion | A/B test messaging, optimize onboarding |
| High churn | Focus on value delivery, improve features |
| API costs spike | Monitor usage, optimize prompts, implement caching |
| Pricing too low | Can raise later, validate with growth |
